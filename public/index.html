        How it works
        1. When it's the people's turn, click a piece and then a square to vote for that move. You can also click a move in the vote list.
        2. The first vote starts a countdown. You can change your vote until it ends.
        3. The move with the most votes is played. Ties go to the move that was suggested first.
        4. Stockfish replies, and the next round starts. After each game the people switch colours and a new game begins.
